As his hometown paper saw him

Roosevelt in the Cow Boy

Packard's paper reported Roosevelt's comings and goings, his stockmen's meetings, hunts and politics, often within days. Roosevelt treated the Cow Boy office as his Medora social headquarters. Every item here is in the paper with his name in print.
