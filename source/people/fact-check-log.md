# People bios: fact-check corrections

An independent pass checked each bio against the full transcribed items.

## Daniel Friedman (`daniel-friedman`)
- 1885-04-30/a53 only says he "expects to return... and will remain at the Sloping bottom ranch the most of the summer"; reworded as a plan, not a fact.

## J. A. Rea (`j-a-rea`)
- Bio said the July 1886 item naming him with Roosevelt was cut off; the full item (1886-07-08/a31) is available and shows Rea and Roosevelt gave the speeches at the Dickinson Fourth of July celebration. Replaced the hedge with the fact and added a moment.
- "H. Teller" corrected to J. H. Teller, territorial secretary (1885-07-09/a47, a16).

## J. D. Garver (`j-d-garver`)
- 1885-04-09/a45 says Mrs. Garver was "quite ill"; 1885-04-16/a41 only feared she "may be seriously ill". Softened "seriously ill".

## H. B. Wadsworth (`h-b-wadsworth`)
- Removed herd size "about 1,800": the figure is an uncertain transcription ("[1,800?]") in 1884-02-07/a30, and 1884-05-08/a17 gives the herd as sixteen hundred.

## J. R. "Russ" Wilson (`j-r-russ-wilson`)
- Bio merged two different letters into "severe weather but small losses". 1885-02-05/a38 says losses would be small; 1885-02-19/a6 says the winter was unusually severe on stock and there might be little need of a round-up "except for hides". Separated them.

## Wal. T. Osterhaut (`wal-t-osterhaut`)
- 1884-05-08/a54: it was cow-horses (forty-seven head brought by Johnny Goodall), not cattle, that were taken to his ranch.

## T. J. Kerr (`t-j-kerr`)
- Bio said that in May 1884 he "moved most of his work to St. Paul". 1884-05-01/a47 and 1884-05-15/a22 say his headquarters would be at Medora with the company's; only the earlier 1884-04-24/a26 spoke of spending most of his time in St. Paul. Corrected.

## C. L. Brinker (`c-l-brinker`)
- His name was signed only to the Northern Pacific time table, not the stage line table printed beside it (e.g. 1884-10-02/a45). Last appearance is 1884-12-04/a26, so "through the end of 1884" became "into December 1884". The depot-roof joke was in August (1884-08-28/a70), not June.

## George W. Harmon (`george-w-harmon`)
- Bio said he "ran sheriff's sales at Medora, including the 1886 foreclosure against John and Mary Kiefer". The Kiefer sale (1886-05-06/a35) was to be held at the court house door in Mandan; only the 1885 Bellows sale (1885-09-17/a34) was near Medora. Also specified that the October 1886 renomination was by the Morton County convention (1886-10-14/a10).

## William H. Francis (`william-h-francis`)
- The Cow Boy's report that he sustained a preliminary injunction in the Ref. Car Co. case (1886-09-02/a9) was corrected by the company's attorneys in 1886-09-09/a8: the suits had been tried and awaited his decision. Removed the retracted claim; added the December pro forma dissolution of the injunction from 1886-12-23/a11.

## Max Bass (`max-bass`)
- No item shows him as a convention delegate; he signed the call (1884-04-03/a3) and was elected to the county central committee (1884-04-17/a7).
- Moment label said "Engaged"; 1884-06-26/a43 calls them "the newly-wedded couple".

## Charles W. Sleeper (`charles-w-sleeper`)
- Bio said the text naming the company is cut off at "N. P."; 1884-02-14/a38 names it in full (N. P. Ref. Car Co.) with the Feb. 5 St. Paul directors' meeting.
- 1885-01-01/a45 reports he departed "Sunday morning", i.e. the last days of December 1884, not the start of 1885.

## Henry S. Boice (`henry-s-boice`)
- 1886-08-26/a27: Willis Eaton nominated him for chairman and the motion "Carried", so he was elected, not merely nominated.
- 1886-04-15/a11 only instructs the association's committee to recommend him for the executive committee (from Dakota) at the Miles City meeting; no item shows him seated on it.

## Vic Smith (`vic-smith`)
- 1885-02-05/a47: illness in his family and bad reports of the exposition "decided him to give up his trip" to New Orleans; he did not go there.

## Dan McKenzie (`dan-mckenzie`)
- "Briefly" is unsupported; 1884-08-14/a32 says he established a Belfield shop that his partner would run while he stayed in Medora.

## J. C. Williamson (`j-c-williamson`)
- Bio said he ran for sheriff in 1886; every 1886 ticket (1886-03-11/a3 etc.) lists him for register of deeds and county clerk, with Fred Willard for sheriff. He was elected in April with 122 votes (1886-04-15/a3).
- Bio and role said he was elected assessor in November 1886; 1886-11-04/a6 lists him as elected county clerk and register of deeds (Willis L. Eaton was assessor). Corrected bio, role and moment label.

## Talleyrand Martin (`talleyrand-martin`)
- "Served as an election judge that July": 1886-07-22/a5 is the July bill for his services as judge at the school-township election; the election date is not given. Removed "that July".
- Clarified "a general" as Gen. W. B. Hazen, chief of the weather bureau (1885-05-21/a41).

## Gilbert A. Pierce (`gilbert-a-pierce`)
- Arbor Day items are from 1885 (1885-03-26/a4, 1885-04-16/a54), not 1886 as the bio implied; quarantine covered Chicago and Illinois cattle (1886-10-07/a18).

## Fred A. Willard (`fred-a-willard`)
- Bio omitted that he was elected sheriff in April 1886 (122 votes, 1886-04-15/a3; bond accepted 1886-05-06/a23) and implied he only ran; also re-elected November 1886 (1886-11-04/a6). Added an election moment.

## J. C. Fisher (`j-c-fisher`)
- Removed the claim that he was usually printed as 'C. Fisher'; the items consistently print J. C. Fisher (or Jno. C. Fisher).

## W. F. Van Driesche (`w-f-van-driesche`)
- Bio said the paper names two men, F. and William Van Driesche, and combined them. The March 1884 letter list is signed "WM. F. VAN DRIESCHE, P. M." (1884-03-13/a33), so they are one man; rewrote accordingly.
- Bio said he ran for county surveyor and was elected coroner in 1886. Every ticket lists him for treasurer (1886-03-11/a3); he was elected treasurer in April (1886-04-15/a3) and November (1886-11-04/a6), and resigned (1886-12-23/a36). Arthur W. Smith was the surveyor nominee and Talleyrand Martin the coroner. Role and moment label corrected.
- Postmaster "from March 1884 through late 1886" ignored that J. C. Fisher signed as postmaster in Jan.–Feb. 1885 (1885-01-01/a14, 1885-02-19/a40); Van Driesche signed as assistant postmaster in Oct.–Nov. 1884 and as postmaster again from 1885-04-30/a43.
- No item shows him as a convention delegate in spring 1884; he signed the convention call (1884-04-03/a3).
- Prairie chicken hunt was in August 1886 (1886-08-19/a41: thirty-three in under two hours); the September item (1886-09-23/a44) only referred back to it.
- "Medora Gun Club" name unsupported in 1885-02-19/a10; changed to "the new gun club".

## Arthur T. Packard (`arthur-t-packard`)
- The only statehood item (1885-12-03/a10) attacks the motives of the southern Dakota statehood movement's leaders; "took on the push for Dakota statehood" misstated it.

## Marquis de Morès (`marquis-de-mores`)
- The 26-hour ride was the return trip from Deadwood to Medora; the ride down took forty-eight hours (1884-04-24/a5). The stage line was agreed to in April, per the same item.
- Alfalfa (1884-06-12/a59) and kaolin (1885-05-07/a53, 1885-05-28/a26) were not October 1884 events, and 'tried out' the kaolin is unsupported; he 'received' (not 'filled') a Chicago order for 500 cattle (1884-10-30/a50).

## Bob Roberts (`bob-roberts`)
- 1885-01-01/a4 only says he and Vic Smith 'will start' for New Orleans; he was still in Medora and started for Wales on Jan. 28 (1885-01-29/a42), and Vic Smith travelled with Bob's buckskin suit (1885-02-05/a47). Departure with Smith is unsupported.
- No item places his building on Main Street (1885-04-16/a42 says only 'Bob Roberts building').

## John W. Goodall (`john-w-goodall`)
- He went to San Antonio on business for the N. P. Ref. Car Co., not the N. P. railroad (1886-12-09/a19).

## Joe Pennell (`joe-pennell`)
- The ride to Deadwood took forty-eight hours, not thirty-six (1884-04-24/a5); thirty-six was only the goal (1884-04-17/a49).
- Staking the route was 'last Saturday' in the May 1 issue, i.e. April 26, 1884 (1884-05-01/a51), not May.
- He left with the mules for the Canadian Pacific on July 16, 1884 (1884-07-17/a26; Seaton letter 1885-03-12/a14), not August.

## Medora de Morès (`medora-de-mores`)
- 1884-09-25/a53 says the corner-stone laying was witnessed by the Marquise, who was building the church; it does not say she laid it.

## Gill Kennedy (`gill-kennedy`)
- He was not a delegate: he signed the convention call (1884-04-03/a3) and was on the committee that chose delegates Ertel and Packard (1884-08-21/a41).
- The January 1885 fire actually burned Paddock's building, which he occupied (1885-01-15/a65); the 'nearly set on fire' attempt was Christmas 1884, before he left (1885-01-01/a41).

## James Hannigan (`james-hannigan`)
- The school board meeting was 'last Saturday' in the Dec. 4, 1884 issue, i.e. Nov. 29 (1884-12-04/a4).

## Mose Ryder (`mose-ryder`)
- The Cannon Ball beef herd (1885-07-16/a52) and the line riders on Ash coulie creek (1885-10-15/a50) were separate items; the bio merged them.

## Fred Banan (`fred-banan`)
- The barn was Paddock's, and the building was Fitzgerald's old dwelling (1885-05-14/a51).
- The paper never says he paid; he said he would serve out the fine rather than pay, and later the total was given as a little over $31 (1886-03-11/a52, 1886-03-18/a36).

## Frank Moore (`frank-moore`)
- Names corrected: E. G. Paddock (1884-02-28/a34), E. W. Casey & Co. (1885-03-26/a24), L. C. Hunt (1886-02-04/a36).

## D. O. Sweet (`d-o-sweet`)
- He ran for county surveyor, not coroner (Talleyrand Martin was the coroner candidate) (1886-03-25/a36, 1886-04-01/a2, 1886-04-15/a3).
- It was Mrs. Malloy, not Mrs. Sweet, who took boarders, in the cottage Sweet had vacated (1886-07-08/a23).

## Joe McCoul (`joe-mccoul`)
- The April 1886 motion did not pass: the roll call was 8-7, the chair voted nay and declared it lost (1886-04-15/a11).

## J. C. Copeland (`j-c-copeland`)
- The jail contract went to J. A. Copeland (1886-11-11/a34).

## D. T. Bramble (`d-t-bramble`)
- 'Dennis' is unsupported as his name: 1884-07-17/a3 ('Mr. Bramble's name is D-E-N-N-I-S') and 1884-07-31/a44 use the period slang 'his name is Dennis' (he is done for); only the initials D. T. are given (1884-07-17/a3, 1884-09-04/a58).

## D. C. Rowe (`d-c-rowe`)
- 1885-03-26/a24 is the association's membership list from its by-laws, not a brand directory.

## Lloyd Roberts (`lloyd-roberts`)
- The buffalo were killed 'one day last week' per the Sept. 4, 1884 issue, i.e. late August (1884-09-04/a53).

## Joe O'Brien (`joe-o-brien`)
- Left for Cheyenne 'last Monday' per the Sept. 3, 1885 issue, i.e. Aug. 31, not September (1885-09-03/a31).

## Wilmot Dow (`wilmot-dow`)
- The pursuit of the boat thieves began in late March 1886 (1886-04-01/a49 reports they had already started; 1886-04-15/a34 says the boat was stolen about three weeks earlier), not April.

## Wadsworth (`wadsworth`)
- Moment label called it the 'first' stockmen's meeting; 1884-12-04/a16 does not say it was the first.

## B. F. Reynolds (`b-f-reynolds`)
- The Sept. 3, 1885 item (1885-09-03/a45) reports the cattle had already arrived and G. W. left 'last week', so B. F. took charge at the end of August, not in September.

## J. E. Hample (`j-e-hample`)
- No item says he bought hides again in 1885; 1885-10-15/a30 has him in Medora in October 1885, and 1885-12-24/a34 says only that he had 'several times' bought the company's hides.

## L. N. Cary (`l-n-cary`)
- Role called him a lawyer; 1884-07-24/a4 names only the firm Carpenter & Cary and his success 'in his own business', not a law practice.
- Moment label 'Nominated for the legislature' implied a party nomination; it was the Cow Boy's own endorsement (1884-07-24/a4).

## William Tolbert (`william-tolbert`)
- The Elk saloon was in Little Missouri, not Medora (1885-06-04/a54: 'the Elk Saloon in Little Missouri').

## J. P. Small (`j-p-small`)
- His transfer visit was 'Saturday' before the Sept. 3, 1885 issue, i.e. Aug. 29 (1885-09-03/a22); the decision not to move the herd came in September (1885-09-24/a18).
- He returned from Chicago 'Sunday' before the Sept. 2, 1886 issue, so the sale was in late August, not September (1886-09-02/a33).

## John S. Seaton (`john-s-seaton`)
- Bio implied the Jones & Pennell outfit was from Yonkers; Seaton wrote from Yonkers about the outfit's work since leaving Medora (1885-03-12/a14).
- 'Judge Seaton's court' appears only in a comic account of a vagrancy trial (1886-06-10/a22, defendant 'Mr. A. 1. Stiff'); no item shows he was a Medora justice, so role and moment were adjusted.

## P. D. Cravath (`p-d-cravath`)
- They left 'Monday last' per the July 3, 1884 issue, i.e. June 30 (1884-07-03/a47), not in July.

## Calamity Joe (`calamity-joe`)
- The escape from the deputy was at Rapid City (1886-02-11/a40), not 'near' it.

## Harvey T. Halleck (`harvey-t-halleck`)
- He was not merely 'named in a legal notice signed by Julius LeMoyne'; the notice (1886-05-13/a38, 1886-06-10/a42) lists H. T. Hallock of Morton County as a partner and signer of the LeMoyne Cattle Co., Limited.

## Margaret Gardient (`margaret-gardient`)
- She did not entertain at an event in March 1885; the Mar. 26 item (1885-03-26/a45) announced the April 1 Stockmen's ball and named her among the hotel's entertainers. The April prize was at a masquerade on skates (1885-04-16/a53).

## Mrs. Paddock (`mrs-paddock`)
- Bio implied 'a Mr. Roberts' arrived from Wales in August 1885; the item says Bob Roberts' sister, who had come with him from Wales, arrived from Bismarck that August (1885-08-20/a35).

## Sheriff Cuskelley (`sheriff-cuskelley`)
- The pony replevin was at Belfield (1884-03-27/a55, Belfield letter), not in the Bad Lands.
- The Standing Rock trip is reported in the Sept. 4, 1884 issue as already completed, so it was not necessarily in September (1884-09-04/a60).

## Dave Brown (`dave-brown`)
- The May 1886 item is not cut off: he and his wife accompanied Mr. and Mrs. Tisdale to Medora (1886-05-13/a26), the newly married Jack Tisdales (1886-05-13/a35). Removed meta remark.

## Harry Bennett (`harry-bennett`)
- He found the horses about sixty miles west of Fort McGinness, not near it, and returned in June after a 21-day trip (1886-06-17/a36).

## Pierce Boland (`pierce-boland`)
- The Feb. 1884 Little Missouri letter (1884-02-28/a48) names only 'Boland', which cannot be tied to Pierce Boland; claim removed.

## Tom Martin (`tom-martin`)
- The Shonkin range drive is a Benton River Press reprint about Montana cattle (1884-05-08/a20) and cannot be tied to the Sully creek Tom Martin; removed.
- The Sully creek dinner was 'last Sunday' before the June 4, 1885 issue, i.e. May 31 (1885-06-04/a68).
- Role 'cattle hand' was unsupported.

## Z. Hyde (`z-hyde`)
- Family arrival dated wrong: 1884-05-01/a46 says he returned from Chicago with wife and children 'Saturday evening', i.e. April 26, 1884; changed May to April.

## Clark (`clark`)
- 1885-03-26/a14 reports only that the cowboys were out on bail in March 1885; the dancing incident itself is undated. Reworded so the date attaches to the report.

## Samuel English (`samuel-english`)
- 1885-04-02/a46 (issue of April 2) says they 'have moved', so the move happened by then, likely in March; changed 'In April 1885 ... moved' to 'By early April 1885 ... had moved'.

## A. Osborne (`a-osborne`)
- 1885-08-27/a27: the disease was at Sentinel Butte; Osborne, who had cattle on Andrews creek, said it was contagious. Bio wrongly placed the deaths on Andrews Creek.

## Chas. S. Fee (`chas-s-fee`)
- 1884-08-21/a39: Fee and Footner signed contracts 'giving Medora exclusive control of the business of their departments'; the item does not say the contracts went to the Medora Stage and Forwarding Co. Corrected bio and moment label.
- 1886-03-18/a22 quotes the new edition of 'Wonderland' but does not say a copy was received; reworded.

## F. H. Eaton (`f-h-eaton`)
- 1886-06-10/a37 does not say he rode in; reworded to 'coming in from the Logging Camp'.

## J. W. Gallup (`j-w-gallup`)
- Sale date wrong: the only source, 1886-02-11/a37, reports in February 1886 that Aleck Connell had bought Gallup's ranch; the bio said 'By September 1885'.
- 1885-03-12/a39: he was to be postmaster at Cave Hills, not Medora; removed ambiguous 'there'.

## Henry L. Dawes (`henry-l-dawes`)
- 1885-02-05/a8 concerns a lease of the Crow reservation for pasture, not a reservation-opening plan; corrected bio and moment label.
- 1885-06-25/a9 says the Dawes party would probably go as far north as the southern limit of the Crow reservation, not visit it; corrected.
- 1885-12-31/a8 mocks his constituents rather than criticizing their policy views in general; tightened wording.

## L. D. Pettit (`l-d-pettit`)
- 1884-09-04/a56 says he 'is erecting' a residence to be ready in about two weeks; completion is not reported.

## Mark Bateman (`mark-bateman`)
- 1884-05-01/a39 and a52 (issue of May 1) report the Magranahan inquest held Tuesday, i.e. April 29, 1884; changed May to April.

## Avery (`avery`)
- 1884-04-24/a20 gives the host's full name, A. M. Sinnott, and says Avery had returned east; the bio wrongly said the surname was cut off and omitted the departure.

## Dr. W. H. Williamson (`dr-w-h-williamson`)
- 1886-05-06/a3: the room-near-the-ballroom claim came from a Bismarck telegram the Cow Boy called a libel with 25 lies; the paper said his room was the farthest from the dancing, only one shot was fired in the hall, and it blamed Williamson for the exaggerated account. Bio had presented the telegram's claim as the paper's report.

## Ferris (`ferris`)
- 1886-09-02/a20 says he 'will accompany' the cattle; the trip itself is not reported.

## Hattie Eaton (`hattie-eaton`)
- 1885-04-23/a65 does not say they came from Pittsburgh (only the 1886 item does); removed from the 1885 claim.

## Janie Fitzgerald (`janie-fitzgerald`)
- Role said she died in Iowa; 1885-07-30/a13 reports her death without giving a place.

## S. W. Russel (`s-w-russel`)
- Sequence wrong: 1885-08-13/a22 reports he had delivered N bar cattle and returned to Miles City; 1885-08-27/a33 reports a later delivery of 400 cattle to the N. P. Ref. Car Co. Bio had the return after the 400-head delivery and placed it 'at Medora', which the items do not state.

## Dan Williams (`dan-williams`)
- Bio said he replaced 'H. Minty'; the item names W. H. Minty (1886-04-01/a46).

## Levi Deffebach (`levi-deffebach`)
- Moment label said he returned 'after settling a family estate'; the item says the trip was not a success and a lawyer had taken the matter in charge (1885-03-26/a41).

## John B. Walker (`john-b-walker`)
- Bio said he took over the Pyramid Park hotel in February 1885; the item says he would take Arch Boyd's place the first of March (1885-02-26/a34).

## T. A. Matthews (`t-a-matthews`)
- Bio dated his Deputy U.S. Marshal appointment to January 1885; the item in the January 8, 1885 issue reports it as already made, so changed to 'by January 1885' (1885-01-08/a35).

## Edward L. Simmers (`edward-l-simmers`)
- Bio said school opened in December 1885; the December 3, 1885 issue says it began 'last Monday', i.e. November 30 (1885-12-03/a42).
- Bio said he 'took charge of the N. P.'; the item says the N. P. Hotel bar (1886-04-01/a38).

## J. Randolph Grymes (`j-randolph-grymes`)
- Bio said 'Bad Lands Gun Club shoots' (plural); he appears in one shoot's scores (1885-03-05/a44).
- Bio said he returned to New York in October 1886; the October 7 issue says he left 'last week', so changed to 'In the fall of 1886' (1886-10-07/a30).

## Tilden R. Selmes (`tilden-r-selmes`)
- Bio placed his stag-hound hunt plans in December 1886; the item is from September 1886 (1886-09-23/a38).

## Frank Keeler (`frank-keeler`)
- Bio said he was a delegate to the April 1884 Republican convention; he was one of the signers of the call for the county convention (1884-04-03/a3).

## George Wilkes (`george-wilkes`)
- Bio said he brought tools from Chicago in May 1884; the May 1 issue says he returned 'last Friday', i.e. April 25 (1884-05-01/a44).
- Bio said he left the company in August 1885; the August 6 issue says 'last week', i.e. late July (1885-08-06/a27).

## Morris Sebastian (`morris-sebastian`)
- Bio and moment said he recovered/returned with the stolen Gallien stock; the item says the stock had been confiscated and sold by the Canadian government and the proceeds were to go to the Galliens (1885-10-29/a39).

## Benjamin F. Lamb (`benjamin-f-lamb`)
- Bio, role and moment said he was nominated for and elected county treasurer; the items show he was nominated and elected probate judge (W. F. Van Driesche was treasurer) (1886-10-28/a49, 1886-11-04/a6).
- Bio said he was 'nominated for membership' in the stockgrowers' association; he was nominated and unanimously elected (1885-09-10/a19).
- Bio put the purchase of 68 M bar cattle in spring 1885; the item is from July 1885 (1885-07-16/a31).

## M. M. Coleman (`m-m-coleman`)
- Moment called it 'Medora's first school'; a school had already run at the Cantonment from July 1884 (1884-11-27/a51; see 1884-07-17/a24).

## C. F. Ellison (`c-f-ellison`)
- Bio said he was ice agent for the Northern Pacific; the item says the N. P. R. C. Co. (Northern Pacific Refrigerator Car Company) (1884-11-06/a57).
- Bio called his wife 'new'; no item says they were newly married (1885-12-17/a31, 1885-12-31/a27).

## R. E. Spurrier (`r-e-spurrier`)

## John S. Warn (`john-s-warn`)
- Bio reported the rumored one-year sentence without the correction in the same item: he was in fact sentenced to thirty days (1885-04-09/a39).
- Bio named the buyer 'W. McDonald'; the item says M. W. McDonald (1885-06-25/a45).
- Bio said he bought the Commercial house 'in July'; the July 2 issue reports it as done, so changed to 'by early July' (1885-07-02/a61).

## Dorr Clark (`dorr-clark`)

## Peter J. Book (`peter-j-book`)

- Bio said he opened a brickyard in Miles City; the items only say he planned one and that he and his brick men went to work on buildings there (1885-06-11/a37, 1885-07-23/a28).

## Dr. H. M. Jorgens (`dr-h-m-jorgens`)
- Bio called Louthan his 'partner'; the items name Dr. J. M. Louthan and Jorgens as two men locating separately (1884-05-01/a53, 1884-05-08/a58).
- Bio said Jorgens urged everyone to be vaccinated; the item says he received fresh vaccine and the paper urged vaccination (1885-12-03/a35).

## J. L. Truscott (`j-l-truscott`)
- Bio said he was elected secretary in April 1885; the report ran in the April 2 issue, so the meeting was earlier; changed to 'spring 1885' (1885-04-02/a7).
- Association name: the paper's own name for it is the Little Missouri River Stockgrowers' Association (1885-09-10/a19).

## Howard Eaton (`howard-eaton`)
- Bio said 'a new cattle company' would put 1,000 head in his charge; the item names two visiting lumbermen, G. F. Randall and A. J. Rich (1884-04-24/a52).

## Theodore Roosevelt (`theodore-roosevelt`)
- Bio dated the report of ~100 ranch-job applications to January 1886; it ran in the February 4, 1886 issue (1886-02-04/a7).
- Moment called the December 1884 meeting the 'first meeting of Little Missouri stockmen'; stockmen had met in February and April 1884; the December meeting formed the association (1884-12-25/a3).

## E. G. Paddock (`e-g-paddock`)
- School board was elected at a meeting 'last Saturday' (Nov. 29, 1884) reported in the Dec. 4 issue, not December (1884-12-04/a4).
- First gun-club shoot was 'last Saturday' (Feb. 28) in the Mar. 5 issue; he also won the Mar. 7 shoot (1885-03-05/a44, 1885-03-12/a46).
- $250 reward was a joint notice by several firms about grass fires, running June 1884-Jan. 1885, not his own standing reward (1884-06-19/a6 to 1885-01-15/a14).
- Lloyd Roberts led the May 1884 cattle purchase with Paddock and Friedman (1884-05-22/a34).
- The case, not Paddock, was 'taken to Bismarck'; added his discharge reported 1885-09-24/a47 so the bio does not leave him under arrest (1885-09-03/a36).
- Feb. 21, 1884 'boom my lots' joke came from a reader's letter (1884-02-21/a51).

## J. N. Maxwell (`j-n-maxwell`)
