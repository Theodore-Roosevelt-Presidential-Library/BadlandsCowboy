mourning kneeling in long, silent
jrayer before one of the side alters.

Vhen at last she rose she looked
about her in search of something which
she missed, and then walked slowly
away, and supporting herself by the
wall, toward the entrance. The
stranger politely offered his arm,
which wa3 gratefully accepted, the
lady meanwhile explaining that one
of
the beggars must nave taken her sil
ver-headed walking-stick away, with

tiring offered his address card (alas,
for cruel Nemesis, he was a German

then return the civility by whispering:
"Empress Eugenie, and—homeless."
