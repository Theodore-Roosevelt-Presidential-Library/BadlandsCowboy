Onr Chicago market report shows a
lowering of prices all around, especially
in grass-fed Texans and western range
cattle, both dropping about fifty cents.
Heavily increased arrivals of all classes
of cattle was the cause of the depression.
The arrivals of Texas and western' cat
tle was 26,000 of which 17,000 were from
Texas and about' 4,000 from Dakota and
Montana. Extra prime beeves were the
only class to escape the general depres
sion, they advancing ten to fifteen cents.
The present outlook would seein to indi
cate that prices will be low for some
time, as many cattle are now being sent
to market that should stay on the range
another year. There is also every chance
that a large number will be marketed
from the Indian Territory and this will
result in another drop.
