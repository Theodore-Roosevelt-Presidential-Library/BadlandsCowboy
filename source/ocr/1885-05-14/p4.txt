Citizens' Meeting.

The meeting last Friday evening was
called to order by Marquis de Mores and
A. T. Packard was elected chairman.

The chairman stated the objects of the
meeting to be, to organize a fire depart
ment, to adopt measures to keep the
town free of all rubbish, to perfect
school organization and to appoint
committee to

so6

that order is kept.

In reference to the fire department tho
chairman -of the old committee, J. C.
Fisher reported that a sum of money,
probably sufficient for present need, had
been subscribed but that no word had as
yet been received from F. D. Foster
relative to material. Further time was
given the committee. Chief of the fire
department were then elected as follows
J. A. Ferris and J. B. Walker on the west
side of the river and A. T.Packard and
Bob. Boberts on the east side.

A report of the old school board by J.
C. Fisher was then given by which it
appeared that about $40.00 was yet due
on last term's expenses. Snfficiont
money had been guaranteed, however,
and the old board was requested to-col
lect the amount due and liquidate all
indebtedness. It was decided to begin
a new torm of school as soon as practi
cable, a new board being appointed,
consisting of H. M. Jorgens, Wm. Van
Driesche and J. A. Ferris.

The cleanliness of the town was then
considered and a committee consisting
of J. C. Fisher, Wm. Van Driesche and
C. F. Brown, was appointed to, request
all citizens io thoroughly clean their
premises and to keep them clean.

As there is no jxranty organization
here means were discussed to preserve
good order and it was decided that-the
fire committee, Messrs. Packard & Bob
bertsshould give their personal attention
to the subject of good order.

No more business coming before the
meeting, it was declared adjonrned.
