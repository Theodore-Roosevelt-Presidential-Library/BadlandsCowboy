County Organization.

A number of .person? have (ately
spoken to us and in fact' tlie hurdlh of
the conversation seems fo be the advis
ability of organizing the county. This
is by no means confined to Medora alone,
but at least half the stockmen in the
county have voluntarily-expressed their
wish that the county be organized pro
vided it does not cost an unreasonable
amouut.

In conversation with sheriff Harmon,
of Morton Co., our eyes were opened on
the subject of expenses. The actual
running expenses of a county are about
as follows: $300 each to the recorder and
treasurer, $130 to the county attorney,
$100 to the assesor, and $3 each per day
to the commissioners while in actual
session. The sheriffs fees will surely
fall short of $1,000. This will make the
running expenses of the county not over
$'2,000. In addition to this, a good set
of county books will cost about $1,2%
and rent of buildings for county offices,
say $600. This will make the expenses
for the first year in the neighborhood of
$4,000. Of this amount, at least $3,COO
and perhaps more will be obtained from
the railroad, leaving less than $400 to be
raised from the county.

Is there a man in the county after this
showing, who does not want to organ
ize? More than this, if we organize
before the tax is collected by Stark Co.
we do not need to pay a cent to that
county. Sheriff Harmon says that this
fact is proved beyond a doubt.

Even if the organization cost the
people of Billings Co., $4,000 instead of
$400 we think there would be no unwil
lingness to organize.

With official power a hundred and fifty
miles away, lifo and property are not
safe. Who would object to paying his
share of $400 or even $4,000 to secure
protection?

An easy problem for each to solte is to
figure the total assessable property at
$900,000. Then say even $4,000 had to
be raised. This would equal a tax of
eight mills. Now figure yonr share and
see if protection to life and property is
not worth the price. After figuring
your share of $4,000, consider that the
tax is liable to be only one-tenth of that
original amount and see if it is profit
able to be at the mercy o&any drunken
ruffian who wants to paint the town red.

All the above figuring does not include
the subject of licenses. Medora is now
paying Morton Co $1,200 per yenr for
license tax. This amount will pay all
the school and road expenses of the
county.

To coacludo, we think that there is
scarcely anyone in the county opposed
to organization. All it redmres is a lit
tle effort In getting the signatures and
the thing is done. We have sworn off
on plans for organization and propose to
let others "run the masheen." We do
not ask for nor want any office whatever*
but propose to bs entirely free to criti
cize any move we see fit.

Stoekmen should see that it is for
thfeir interest and themselves start the
tnll ro'ling.
