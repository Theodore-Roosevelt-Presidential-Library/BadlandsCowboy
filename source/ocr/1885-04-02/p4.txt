cerned. It was the worst shooting we
have yet had, the only redeeming feature
being Peter Book's hundred yard score.

The day was almost perfoct for shootr
ing, the weather fine and hardly a breath
of air stirring. Almost all the contest
ants claimed to bo in good trim for
shooting and then would, stop up and
miss the target. There, is something
about shooting which cannot be ex
plained and still it .will make a whole
team shoot: poorly. We cannot imagine
what it is, but below is seen the result.
It might be added that the target is the
regulation' army one'of an eight-inch
bulls eye at the three ranges.
