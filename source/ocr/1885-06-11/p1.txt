There has probably never been a time
in the history of thorough-bred cattle in
the United States, when they could be
purchased at such low figures as at the
present time. There is no reason now
Why range cattle should not be bred to a
higher standard. The objection may be
advanced, that the higher bred stock will
not stand privation because it is not
invested with as much vitality as
natives. This objection is undoubtedly
to some extent well founded, as that is an
end that has not been sought by breeders
to any great extent up to the present
time. The man who produces an animal
especially adapted to the range, and
popular in the eastern markets will have
a fortune at his command. An animal
possessing the instincts of the Texas
cow, and the body of nearly any of the
high bred cattle barring .the Jersey,
would very nearly meet the demand.
