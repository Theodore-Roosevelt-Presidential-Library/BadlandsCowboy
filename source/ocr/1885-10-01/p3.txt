Any one who has ever visited Kings
ton, Canada, will recall the round
towers which form a part of its ex
tensive fortifications. These, it is sup
posed, were copied from the similar
structures on the coast of Ireland,
which are about to be demolished.
The Irish towers were built at the
time when Lord Cornwallis was Vice
roy of Ireland, at the suggestion of the
Buke of Richmond, who had heard
that the town of Martello, in Corsica,
had by means of similar defenses suc
cessfully resisted the attack of a fleet.
This Duke of Richmond afterward be
came Governor General of Canada,
and it was during his career that these
Martello towers were built for Kings
ton, then the capital ot the united
provinces of Upper and Lower Can
ada. His deatn occurred in 1819
from hydrophobia, produced by the
bite of a fox.
