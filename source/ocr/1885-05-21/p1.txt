A point that seems to have attracted
but little attention in the Bod Lands is
the subject of the laws on branding.
Whether there are any in Dakota seems
to be entirely unknown by our stockmen,
all seeming to depend on the association
rules on the subject. It is unfortunate
however, that the headquarters of the
association are in Montana and that the
territorial laws which apply to the Mon
tana association do not by any means
apply to Dakota cattle. Our cowmen
should see to it that laws relative to
branding be passed in Dakota similar to
those in force in Montana. As it uow is
all the cattlemen are members of the
Montana association and governed by its
laws while legally none of those laws
apply here. This is a topic for discus
sion at the next meeting of the Little
Missouri association.
